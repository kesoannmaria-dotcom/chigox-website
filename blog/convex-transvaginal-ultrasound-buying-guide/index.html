Convex Transvaginal Ultrasound Buying Guide

A practical guide for gynecology clinics, hospitals, distributors and importers planning handheld convex and transvaginal ultrasound configurations.

Convex transvaginal ultrasound purchasing is a workflow decision as much as a product decision. Gynecology clinics, hospitals, outpatient departments, distributors and importers need to define the examinations they want to support, the display platform, accessory package, training workflow and quotation details before choosing a configuration.

Start with the Examination Mix

The CHIGOX Convex Transvaginal Scanner category lists the CGU-03 Wireless Handheld Convex & Transvaginal Ultrasound Scanner. The product page describes CGU-03 as a wireless dual-head handheld ultrasound scanner integrating a convex probe and a transvaginal probe into a single compact device.

For buyers, the first question is whether the planned workflow needs both abdominal and pelvic access from one handheld scanner. The CGU-03 page lists gynecology, pelvic examination, uterus assessment, ovarian evaluation, obstetrics, early pregnancy assessment, fetal monitoring, prenatal examination, general imaging and abdominal ultrasound.

Confirm Probe Strategy

The CGU-03 page lists the probe type as Convex + Transvaginal. That makes the product relevant for buyers who want one handheld scanner discussion covering both convex and transvaginal use cases.

Distributors should still confirm how the buyer separates examination rooms, cleaning workflow, probe cover needs and department ownership. A combined device can simplify product selection, but it does not replace the need to define who will use it, where it will be used and which accessories are expected in the first quotation.

Review Imaging Modes and Display Devices

The CGU-03 technical table lists imaging modes including B, B/M, Color Doppler, PW and PDI. It also lists smartphone, tablet and PC display devices, with Android, iOS and Windows operating systems.

Before requesting a quote, buyers should confirm whether the department plans to use existing phones, tablets or computers, or whether the quotation should include display-related accessories. The product page lists tablet mount as an optional accessory, so display planning should be part of the purchasing conversation.

Plan Wireless Operation and Daily Handling

The CGU-03 page lists wireless WiFi connection, compact handheld design, rechargeable lithium battery and portable imaging for gynecology, obstetrics, abdominal examinations and point-of-care applications. Its standard configuration includes wireless ultrasound probe, charging cable, carrying case, user manual and ultrasound software.

For clinics and importers, this creates practical questions: where the scanner will be charged, how the device will be stored, whether a carrying case is enough for the workflow, and whether sterile probe covers or a tablet mount should be included from the beginning.

Compare Nearby Handheld Ultrasound Options

CHIGOX lists CGU-03 inside the broader Handheld Ultrasound category. Nearby handheld options include convex, linear, convex linear, phased array and 3-in-1 scanner categories, each aimed at different application mixes.

Buyers focused on gynecology, obstetrics and pelvic workflows may start with CGU-03. Buyers needing vascular, musculoskeletal, cardiac or broader multi-probe workflows should compare the relevant handheld ultrasound category pages before finalizing an inquiry.

Build a Clear Quotation Brief

A stronger convex transvaginal ultrasound inquiry gives CHIGOX enough context to discuss the current configuration and accessories. This is especially useful for distributors and importers collecting requests from gynecology clinics or hospital departments.

Useful details include:

  • Buyer type, such as gynecology clinic, hospital, outpatient department, distributor or importer
  • Main applications, such as gynecology, obstetrics, pelvic examination or abdominal ultrasound
  • Display plan, such as smartphone, tablet or PC
  • Operating system needs, such as Android, iOS or Windows
  • Accessory needs, including carrying case, tablet mount, wireless charger or sterile probe covers
  • Quantity range, destination country, timeline and documentation questions

Where CHIGOX Fits

CHIGOX lists CGU-03 under Ultrasound and Handheld Ultrasound, alongside other handheld ultrasound configurations. Buyers can review the Convex Transvaginal Scanner category and CGU-03 product page before requesting a configuration discussion.

Next Step for Buyers

Before requesting a quote, define the application mix, display device, operating system, accessory package, quantity range and destination market. Then ask CHIGOX to confirm the current convex transvaginal ultrasound configuration for your project.
