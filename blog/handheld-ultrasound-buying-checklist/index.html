What to Look for When Buying a Handheld Ultrasound Scanner

A practical buying guide for clinics, procurement teams and distributors comparing handheld ultrasound options.

A handheld ultrasound buying guide should help buyers compare more than a headline price. If you receive quotations from several suppliers, compare the configuration, probe requirement, display workflow, included items, warranty, support information and available documents before deciding which quote offers the best fit.

This guide is for clinics, physicians, procurement teams and distributors evaluating a handheld ultrasound scanner. It focuses on the practical questions to answer before requesting or comparing quotations.

What should a clinic define before requesting a handheld ultrasound quotation?

Start by defining what you need the scanner to support in your own working environment.

Before contacting suppliers, clarify:

  • What you plan to use the device for
  • Whether you need one probe format or more than one
  • Who is buying the device: a clinic, hospital department, distributor or another organization
  • Which country the equipment will be supplied to
  • Whether the purchase is for one unit, several units or a future distribution plan
  • When you expect to make a purchasing decision

These answers make a quotation request more useful. They also prevent a common problem: comparing several quotes that cover different configurations.

If you are still deciding between linear, convex and phased array probe formats, read the CHIGOX Handheld Ultrasound Probe Types guide. This article does not repeat that comparison; it explains what buyers should compare once they begin speaking with suppliers.

What configuration questions should buyers ask?

Ask each supplier to explain the exact configuration being quoted. Do not assume that two devices with similar names include the same probe formats, accessories, software or support.

Useful questions include:

  • Which model is being quoted?
  • Which probe format or formats are included?
  • Is the quoted product a single-format, dual-format or multi-format configuration?
  • Which accessories are included as standard?
  • Which accessories are optional?
  • What software is included, and what details should be confirmed before purchase?
  • Which items are excluded from the quotation?

The CHIGOX Handheld Ultrasound category provides a starting point for reviewing available scanner options by probe configuration. A final decision should always be based on the exact quotation and current product information.

How should buyers assess display and connectivity requirements?

A handheld ultrasound scanner is part of a workflow, not only a probe. Buyers should consider how the scanner will be displayed, used and managed in their own setting.

Before selecting a wireless ultrasound scanner, confirm:

  • Which device will be used for viewing and operating the scanner
  • Whether the clinic expects to use phones, tablets, computers or a combination
  • Whether the scanner will remain in one department or move between locations
  • Whether the team needs clarification on image or video storage and transfer
  • Whether the buyer has internal IT or device-management requirements

Do not rely on a general assumption that any mobile-device workflow will fit every clinic. Ask the supplier to confirm the current configuration, listed software and supported workflow for the quoted model.

Which specifications and documents should be compared?

The best comparison is usually based on the same questions for every supplier.

Request the available product information, then compare the scope of each offer. This may include a specification sheet, user documentation, configuration list, warranty information, available support information and optional-accessory list.

The goal is simple: make sure the quotations are comparable before you compare prices.

A lower quotation is not automatically a better offer if it covers fewer items, a different configuration or a different support scope.

When should a clinic choose a single-format or multi-format configuration?

Choose the configuration that fits the needs you have already defined.

A single-format option can make sense when the buyer has a clear and focused probe requirement. A multi-format option may be worth reviewing when one clinic, department or distributor needs to consider more than one listed probe format.

CHIGOX currently lists the CGU-LC Wireless Convex & Linear Ultrasound Scanner as a convex-and-linear configuration option. The CGU-LCP Wireless 3-in-1 Handheld Ultrasound Scanner is listed with convex, linear and phased array formats.

These pages are useful starting points. Ask CHIGOX to confirm the current configuration and quotation scope for your requirement.

What should buyers confirm for their destination market?

The same handheld ultrasound scanner may require different purchasing documents or import checks depending on the destination country.

Before placing an order, confirm:

  • Destination country
  • Buyer or importer details
  • Any documents required by your internal purchasing process
  • Any local import, registration or labeling questions that require review
  • Who will confirm destination-market requirements before shipment

This is not a reason to delay every inquiry. It is a reason to raise the destination country early, so the supplier can respond to the right questions.

Handheld ultrasound procurement checklist

Before requesting a quotation, check that you can answer the following:

  1. What will the scanner be purchased for?
  2. Which probe format or configuration do you prefer?
  3. Who is the buyer: clinic, hospital, distributor or another organization?
  4. What is the destination country?
  5. What is your expected purchasing timeline?
  6. What needs to be included in the quotation?
  7. Which accessories should be included or quoted separately?
  8. What display and connectivity workflow does your team need?
  9. Which product documents, warranty details and support information do you need to review?
  10. Are you comparing the same scope across all supplier quotations?

Frequently asked buying questions

What should I compare when I receive handheld ultrasound quotations from several suppliers?

Compare the quoted configuration, included and optional items, display and connectivity workflow, available documents, warranty, support information and total quotation scope. Compare prices only after confirming that the offers cover the same requirement.

Should I choose a multi-format handheld ultrasound configuration?

Only if your purchasing need covers more than one listed probe format. Start with the requirement, then review the available configuration rather than assuming a broader option is always necessary.

What information should I provide when requesting a CHIGOX quotation?

Share your destination country, buyer type, preferred probe or configuration, purchasing needs and expected timeline. This helps CHIGOX prepare a more relevant configuration review and quotation.
