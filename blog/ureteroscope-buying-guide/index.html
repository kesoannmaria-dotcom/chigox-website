Ureteroscope Buying Guide for Urology Clinics

A practical guide for urology departments, clinics, hospitals, distributors and importers comparing reusable and disposable flexible ureteroscope configurations.

Choosing a ureteroscope is a workflow decision, not only a model decision. Urology departments, hospitals, ambulatory surgery centers, distributors and importers need to define how the scope will be used, which accessories are required, how imaging will be displayed, and whether reusable or disposable use is a better operational fit.

Start with the Urology Workflow

The CHIGOX Ureteroscope category describes disposable and reusable flexible ureteroscopes for urinary tract diagnosis and minimally invasive treatment. The listed applications across the reusable and disposable pages include flexible ureteroscopy, ureteral stone diagnosis, kidney stone examination, laser lithotripsy, ureteral biopsy, urinary tract inspection, upper urinary tract examination or intervention, and minimally invasive urological surgery.

Before requesting a quotation, buyers should explain whether the project is for a hospital urology department, minimally invasive surgery center, ambulatory surgery center, distributor catalog, import tender or replacement purchase. That context helps CHIGOX discuss the scope, processor, monitor, workstation and accessory package in practical terms.

Compare Reusable and Disposable Paths

The CGU-750R Series Reusable Flexible Ureteroscope is presented as a reusable flexible ureteroscope for routine and advanced upper urinary tract procedures. Its page lists a reusable medical-grade insertion tube, waterproof cap, leak tester, cleaning brush, sterilization cap and optional accessories including an HD endoscopy processor, medical monitor, endoscopy workstation, medical trolley, stone retrieval basket, biopsy forceps, irrigation pump and sterilization tray.

The CGU-650D Series Disposable Flexible Ureteroscope is presented as a sterile single-use flexible ureteroscope for minimally invasive urinary tract diagnosis and treatment. Its page lists sterile packaging and optional accessories including an endoscopy video processor, medical monitor, endoscopy workstation, medical trolley, stone retrieval basket, biopsy forceps, irrigation pump and light source if required by the system.

Reusable configurations may suit buyers who can manage cleaning, sterilization, storage, repair planning and repeated clinical use. Disposable configurations may suit buyers prioritizing sterile single-use workflow, predictable inventory planning and reduced reprocessing steps. The right choice depends on real procedure volume, staffing, infection-control policy, device handling process and procurement model.

Check Size, Channel and Working Length Needs

The CGU-650D disposable page lists model options CGU-650D20, CGU-650D22, CGU-650D25 and CGU-650D28. Listed outer diameters are 2.0 mm, 2.2 mm, 2.5 mm and 2.8 mm; listed working channels are 0.8 mm or 1.2 mm; and listed working length is 650 mm. The page also lists a 90 degree field of view, 2-50 mm depth of field, bidirectional tip deflection and 400 x 400 image resolution for those disposable models.

The CGU-750R reusable page lists a 750 mm working length, 90 degree field of view, 2-50 mm depth of field, Up275 degrees / Down275 degrees tip deflection and HD digital imaging. Its outer diameter and working channel cells say to refer to product specifications, so buyers should confirm the current reusable size options directly with CHIGOX before preparing a final purchase comparison.

Size and channel details should be matched to the intended urology workflow and accessory plan. If the project involves stone retrieval baskets, biopsy forceps, irrigation or laser lithotripsy planning, the inquiry should include accessory expectations so CHIGOX can discuss compatible configurations.

Confirm Processor and Imaging Compatibility

Both ureteroscope product pages state compatibility with endoscopy video or HD endoscopy processors. The related CGE-100 Full HD Medical Endoscopy Solution page describes a platform with video processor, LED cold light source, monitor and optional workstation software. It also states that one processor can support multiple flexible and rigid endoscopes.

For hospitals and distributors, the scope discussion should therefore include processor compatibility, monitor needs, trolley requirements, recording requirements, data export, reporting and whether the buyer is planning a broader endoscopy system rather than a single scope purchase.

Plan Accessories Before Asking for Price

A ureteroscope quotation can change depending on whether the buyer needs only the scope or a fuller endoscopy setup. CHIGOX product pages mention processor, monitor, workstation, trolley, stone retrieval basket, biopsy forceps and irrigation pump options. The reusable page also lists cleaning and sterilization-related items, while the disposable page emphasizes sterile packaging.

Hospitals should clarify which accessories are already available and which must be included. Distributors should ask end customers about current processors, desired display setup, recording workflow, accessory consumption, reprocessing capacity and documentation expectations before submitting a final inquiry.

Build a Clear Quotation Brief

A useful ureteroscope inquiry should include the clinical and purchasing context before asking for pricing. This reduces follow-up questions and helps CHIGOX discuss a configuration aligned with the buyer's actual workflow.

Useful details include:

  • Buyer type, such as hospital, urology department, ambulatory surgery center, distributor or importer
  • Main application, such as ureteroscopy, stone diagnosis, laser lithotripsy, biopsy or urinary tract inspection
  • Reusable or disposable preference, or a request to compare both paths
  • Known diameter, working channel and working length needs
  • Processor, monitor, workstation and trolley requirements
  • Accessory questions, such as stone retrieval basket, biopsy forceps and irrigation pump
  • Quantity range, destination country, purchasing timeline and documentation questions
