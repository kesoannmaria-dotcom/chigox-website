# CGV-DR07 Portable Handheld Veterinary X-ray System

## Basic Information

**Product Name:** Portable Handheld Veterinary X-ray System

**Model:** CGV-DR07

**Product Category:** Veterinary Digital Imaging System


# Product Overview

The CGV-DR07 Portable Handheld Veterinary X-ray System is designed for small animal diagnostic imaging in veterinary clinics, hospitals and mobile veterinary practices.

With an ultra-lightweight 1.8 kg handheld design, high-frequency X-ray generation technology and intelligent image processing, the system provides convenient and efficient radiographic solutions for dogs, cats and other small animals.

The compact design allows veterinarians to perform quick examinations in clinics, treatment rooms and mobile environments.
